# Collector Module Context

Last reviewed: 2026-08-25
Status: Canonical module runtime; backend aliases are retired and browser URLs remain unchanged.

## Purpose and current scope

- Assign collectors to service areas and report assignment/collection state.
- Provide collector-specific customer/payment options and next-due resolution.
- Capture field payments and support receipt reprinting.
- Persist Android collector reschedules, retain their schedule history for Admin audit, and provide branch-scoped Admin create/edit/delete management that syncs only active assignments to the assigned collector device.
- Persist branch-scoped priority-client collection assignments created by Admin, cache the shared queue offline on every collector's Android dashboard, and retain cancellation/payment/expiration audit history plus the actual collector identity on resulting payment or reschedule records.
- Maintain branch-scoped, reversible Excluded Clients controls that remove selected accounts from Collector App work queues without deleting customer, billing, payment, schedule, priority, or audit records.
- Manage approval queues and approve/reject collected entries.
- Automatically group synced collector payments into daily remittances and provide branch-scoped Admin confirmation/rejection with payment-level approval and audit details.
- Present collection history and reports.

## Backend and APIs

- `backend/index.js` is the lazy module descriptor loaded by `server.js` through `core/runtime/module-loader`.
- `backend/collectors.js`: assignment CRUD/reporting under `/api/collectors`, plus Admin-only active exclusion list/create/restore routes under `/exclusions`. Exclude and restore accept multi-account selections, are branch-scoped, require no manually entered reason, and automatically retain the Admin identity, action, selected accounts, and time audit history. Monthly collection reporting attributes each positive, approved `payment` credit to the collector identity that recorded it, includes prepaid and postpaid customers, and excludes pending/rejected entries, adjustments, debits, and remittance state. Current area assignments affect area presentation only and never reassign historical collector totals.
- `backend/collector-client-exclusions.js`: owns the `collector_client_exclusions` store and the shared active-account filter consumed by Collector authentication, priority, receipt, and schedule downloads. Exclusion is a visibility overlay only; it never edits canonical customer or financial stores.
- `backend/collector-payments.js`: options, reprints, payment submission, approvals, and remittances under `/api/collector/payments`. Collector submissions are written as `pending_approval` and are excluded by Billing's canonical effective-entry filter until an Admin approves them. GCash submission itself never claims imported history. Approval of an explicitly GCash row requires one unlocked incoming official credit with the exact/unique-numeric reference, amount, and calendar date; any payment whose reference matches official history is gated even if it was mislabeled Cash. The server claims shared history before the pending-to-approved write, finalizes the existing entry ID afterward, blocks a Temp/Main owner, retains ambiguous claims for deterministic retry, and serializes concurrent approvals. Explicit-ID batch approval can recover an approved-but-unfinalized row without creating another payment or review. Exact submission retries remain idempotent; conflicting reuse returns `409`. MySQL approval locks and changes the pending row plus its reviewer audit in one transaction. Rejection requires a reason, never affects the canonical balance, and releases only this entry's matching unposted claim after the rejection is durable; release resolves the canonical official reference so a short leading-zero variant cannot orphan the reservation. Successful uploads are idempotently grouped by collector and collection day into pending remittances. Customer-payment decisions and cash remittance remain separate controls. Admins may archive only completed or rejected remittances through replay-safe archive/restore actions; archiving never changes payment or financial records.
- `backend/collector-reschedules.js`: authenticated reschedule upload/list/paid-resolution and Admin schedule CRUD mounted under `/api/collector/payments/reschedules`. Collector uploads are idempotent by collector/client record ID, validated against customer-area assignments, and never trust a collector identity from the request body. Every schedule read reconciles active records against Billing's canonical ending balance; zero-balance records become audited `Paid` schedule history and therefore disappear from active Admin/Android queues without deleting the audit record. A Collector list request is always forced to an authoritative active-only snapshot, including when an older APK sends `status=all`; inactive rows are represented only by branch/collector-scoped identifier tombstones, while Admin `status=all` and `status=history` retain the complete audit records. The explicit paid-resolution endpoint independently rechecks the same canonical balance and rejects premature client-side resolution. Partial-payment follow-ups must link to the collector's canonical payment entry and preserve the payment reference, paid amount, remaining balance, date, preferred time, optional note, and audit history. Admin `POST` requires an active Collector account assigned to the selected customer area; Admin `PUT`/`PATCH /:id` changes schedule details without reassigning customer/collector, and partial-payment edits are limited to the follow-up date, preferred time, and optional note; Admin `DELETE /:id` creates a `Deleted` history record whose minimal tombstone lets Android Sync remove the cached active reminder.
- `backend/collector-priorities.js`: Admin-only create/edit/cancel plus Admin/collector list access mounted under `/api/collector/payments/priorities`. Admin `POST` accepts one `accountNumber` or up to 200 unique `accountNumbers` and saves the batch atomically after branch, duplicate, and canonical unpaid-balance validation. Assignments use `assignmentScope: all_collectors`; every collector in the branch receives the same queue without an area or named-collector restriction. It enforces one active assignment per client, Urgent/High/Normal/Low levels, a collection date, optional expiration, and a required reason. Canonical Billing ending balance is snapshotted on reads and writes; fully paid assignments automatically become audited `Paid` history, expired records become `Expired`, and Admin deletion becomes retained `Cancelled` history. These records never alter bills, payments, balances, approval state, or area assignments.
- `backend/collector-next-due.js`: resolves account collection timing and is consumed directly by Admin authentication/information flows.
- `backend/collector-live-updates.js`: provides the authenticated, branch-scoped Server-Sent Events stream exposed as `GET /api/collectors/events`. Successful Collector mutations publish topic-only invalidation events for approvals, remittances, priorities, reschedules, or assignments; failed writes publish nothing, no payment/customer payload is carried in the stream, and per-branch versions let a reconnecting page detect missed changes safely.
- `backend/routes/collectors.js`: retained dormant relational route implementation; it is not mounted by `server.js` and is available only at its canonical module path.
- The former four repository-root/`routes` backend shims were retired in Phase 11.
- Collector login/session/map/transaction endpoints are implemented in Admin-owned `auth.js` under `/api/auth`.
- Admin-owned Collector login, `/collector-me`, `/collector-transactions`, payment-record, and map flows apply the Collector-owned exclusion filter before building or returning assigned-customer records. The Android app replaces its saved `customers` array from this authoritative response on login, foreground refresh, dashboard refresh, manual Sync, and background Sync.

All API prefixes, collector/admin authentication requirements, feature gates, and response contracts remain unchanged by the physical migration.

Visibility changes no longer trigger a whole-workspace request. Events are coalesced, retain current filters/pages, and wait without discarding their topics while the page is hidden, a dialog or field edit is active, a payment/batch/area selection is in progress, or another refresh is running. A 30-second whole-workspace poll runs only while live updates are unavailable; per-branch stream versions trigger one reconciliation after a reconnect only when events were missed. Manual Refresh buttons remain available, transient background failures retain the last rendered data, and the page header reports live/fallback/paused/last-updated state. Each Collector Assignment card keeps **Collected This Month** scoped to that collector's approved payments by actual recorder identity, independent of current assigned areas. The separate Collector Area Assignment guidance card is removed; its Assign Areas workflow remains available from the Collector Assignment header. Customer Payment Approval clearly states that balances remain unchanged until approval, keeps the total/count/refresh controls in one compact header, hides client/reference, collector, and payment-date filters behind an explicit filter button, preserves Clear Filters, uses oldest-first collapsible collector groups and two-day warning treatments, supports per-payment selection with Approve Selected and collector-level Approve All, shows only client/date/amount in each compact entry, and paginates five payments per page. Collector Cash Remittance provides Pending/Remitted/Rejected/Archived filters, approved-cash and payment-status summaries, an explicit-close review modal, required-reason rejection, reviewer audit display, and compact Archive/Restore actions. Only completed or rejected records can be archived; pending work remains visible. Each assignment card shows collector identity, assigned-area and monthly-collection metrics, up to three area badges plus overflow count, a warning treatment for unassigned collectors, and explicit View Details/Edit controls. Collector search appears only when more than five collectors exist and matches collector identity or assigned area without changing the global KPI totals. Rescheduled Clients keeps Create/Refresh actions in its section header and adds a compact Schedule Filters toggle with an active-filter count; Search, Collector, Status, Schedule Date, and Clear remain collapsed by default, selected values persist while collapsed, and Clear stays inside the expanded panel. Its desktop table fits the primary column with fixed 14/22/16/26/12/10 percent Schedule/Client/Collector/Details/Status/Actions widths, compact cells, two-line detail clamping, ellipsis tooltips, and narrow centered status/actions; it converts records to cards below tablet width. ## Data and dependencies

- Canonical storage, database, and role imports come from `core/`.
- Reschedules use the `collector_followups` data-store key, which writes `data/collector_followups.json` in JSON mode and the protected `app_store` row in MySQL mode.
- Priority assignments use the `collector_priority_assignments` data-store key in the same JSON/protected `app_store` abstraction. Each record stores branch/client/area snapshots, `all_collectors` scope, priority, collection and optional expiration dates, required reason, canonical balance snapshot/check time, lifecycle state, Admin identities, timestamps, and append-only audit history.
- Collector App exclusions use the `collector_client_exclusions` data-store key with a versioned branch map. Each account keeps one reversible record, current active/Admin snapshots, timestamps, the automatic `Admin decision` audit label, and a capped append-only exclude/restore audit history. Full-system backup discovers the store automatically; factory reset clears it without affecting preserved Admin/configuration data.
- A reschedule stores its server/client IDs, branch, customer/account/area, assigned collector identity, visit result, schedule date/time, notes, lifecycle status, source, creator/updater/deleter audit fields, and timestamps. Partial-payment follow-ups additionally store their immutable canonical payment ID/reference, payment status when scheduled, paid amount, remaining balance, separate optional collector note, and append-only change history. A newer schedule archives the prior active schedule for that account; paid resolution and Admin deletion archive active schedules. Full inactive records remain in Admin history; Collector responses expose only their server/client IDs and removal time as tombstones, so customer, note, and audit details are not copied into the device cache.
- Depends directly on migrated Admin accounts/roles and collector authentication.
- Depends on Customer Management for customer identity, area, and account number.
- Depends directly on migrated Billing for canonical payment records, entry numbering, balances, and service refresh.
- Official-reference approval additionally depends on Billing's branch-scoped `gcash_transaction_history` claim/finalize queue. Pending Collector rows remain outside that assignment until Admin approval; posted ownership stores the existing Collector payment entry ID rather than inserting a second ledger row.
- JSON mode retains approval/rejection audit fields on the collector payment entry. Relational mode stores the same decision in the Collector-owned `collector_payment_reviews` table, created on first review, while `payment_entries.status` remains Billing's effective-entry gate.
- Collector client review uses Billing's canonical Complimentary flag: active exempt subscribers are labeled Complimentary and excluded from area unpaid totals so they are not treated as collection targets. Other collector payment/reschedule contracts are unchanged.
- Collector browser-side monthly/detail reports treat Billing's `pending_gcash_verification` status as non-reportable, matching the canonical ledger gate so an unverified Admin GCash entry cannot inflate collected amounts before imported proof is bound.
- Remittance and approval data may be consumed by Finance reporting.

## Known risks and follow-up

- Payment capture and approval must remain idempotent and preserve entry numbering. References are still required; stable client payment IDs improve retry identity, while matching legacy retries remain supported for the current Android app.
- Collector authentication routes live outside the module and require Admin/Integration coordination.
- Android releases older than version 1.1 keep reschedules only on-device and cannot populate the Admin table. Version 1.1 uploads immediately when online and retries pending records during assigned-record synchronization.
- The Android offline queue remains device-local until connectivity and a valid collector token are available.
- Admin-created changes appear on Android only after the assigned collector logs in or Sync runs. Android 1.25 requests active schedules, replaces its server-backed active cache only after a complete successful snapshot, consumes tombstones as deletions, and hides old local schedule-history rows; offline unsynced active work is preserved until the server accepts or rejects it. Older APKs no longer receive full history from the hardened API, but upgrading to 1.25 is required to purge already-cached history correctly.
- Paid schedule cleanup fails safe: if Billing's canonical balance cannot be loaded, the schedule remains active and the explicit resolution endpoint returns a retryable verification error rather than trusting a device balance.
- Priority assignments are replaced from the branch-wide server active list only after a successful Android Sync; a failed request preserves the last device cache. New batches, cancellation, expiration, and full payment appear or disappear independently on each collector device after that device syncs. Concurrent offline collection by different collectors remains an operational risk, but the actual submitting collector is retained on canonical payment/reschedule records and the payment approval/duplicate safeguards remain authoritative.
- Exclusion changes are server-authoritative but reach an offline device only after its next successful login or Sync. The server omits excluded customers/priorities/receipts, sends active excluded schedules as removal tombstones, and still accepts already-captured offline payment/reschedule uploads so records made before exclusion are not lost. Restoring visibility makes still-active server schedules/priorities eligible on the next Sync.
- Verify permissions for collector versus admin operations after any route change.
- Repository-root backend aliases must not be recreated.
- Add authenticated end-to-end capture, approval, rejection, remittance, reprint, and idempotency tests before changing financial behavior.
- A GCash Collector payment cannot become effective until its official PDF credit exists and matches. If finalization is interrupted after approval, the reference intentionally remains reserved and the same explicit approval ID must be retried; it must never be recreated or released speculatively.
